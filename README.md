## ᚠ Overview

**Yggdrasil** transforms a traditional resume into a navigable 3D cosmos suspended around the World Tree. Visitors float in the void between seven realms connected by shimmering Bifröst light-bridges—orbiting, zooming, and flying between worlds to uncover background, skills, projects, education, certifications, and contact channels.

Built entirely in **vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** with zero frameworks or build tools, the project runs directly in the browser (`file://`) and deploys seamlessly to **GitHub Pages** (`https://kanak0091.github.io/Yggdrasil-Protfolio/`).

## ᚦ Key Features

- **Procedural 3D Cosmos & Yggdrasil:** Custom GLSL aurora sky dome, three-layer parallax particle fields (stars, frost snow, rising embers), instanced floating monoliths, shooting stars, and a glowing procedural World Tree.
- **Custom Post-Processing Pipeline:** Uses Three.js `EffectComposer` and `UnrealBloomPass` alongside ACES Filmic tone mapping, vignette, and film grain.
- **Smooth Camera Choreography:** Custom spherical orbit controls with mouse parallax, scroll/pinch zoom limits, eased flight trajectories along Bifröst bridges, and selection ember bursts.
- **Tactile HUD & Accessibility:** Interactive Rune Circle Mini-Map, keyboard shortcuts (`ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`, `Enter`, `Esc`), procedural Web Audio soundscape, `prefers-reduced-motion` support, and one-click **Text View**.
